    Seasonal greens, tomatoes, roots, herbs, and fruits.
  • 🥩 Goat Meat
    Pasture-raised cuts—whole, half, or individual portions.
  • 🥚 Farm Eggs
    Free-range chickens with rich, orange yolks.
  • 🥛 Dairy
    Fresh milk, cream, butter, and cheese from pasture-raised cows.

Experience Our Ecolodge – Authentic Farm Immersion Opening soon

  • 🌄 Wake with the Farm
    You’ll rise to exotic birdsong, cool morning air, and the farm stirring awake.
  • 🌾 Immerse in Farm Life
    You’ll milk a cow, watch honey gathered from the comb, and join in tending the vibrant garden.
  • 🍽️ Taste the Farm
    You’ll savor vine-warm tomatoes, just-set cheeses, and honey tasting of wildflowers.
  • 🌿 Reconnect with Nature
    You’ll meditate as sunrise and sunset glow over beautiful Lake Hawassa in the distance.
